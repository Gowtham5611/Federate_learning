**Target Dataset**: `data/processed/ibm_aml/Combined/`  
**Audit Purpose**: Independent verification of origin, construction protocol, chronological integrity, schema compatibility, and overlap with individual datasets.

## 1. Construction Protocol & Origin

The Combined dataset was generated by [`backend/scripts/create_combined_dataset.py`](file:///c:/Users/Acer/OneDrive/Desktop/Federate_learning/backend/scripts/create_combined_dataset.py) to provide a composite benchmark uniting both the high-illicit (`HI-Small`) and low-illicit (`LI-Medium`) IBM AML distributions.

### Split Composition
1. **Train Split**:
   - Source 1: First 150,000 transactions of `data/processed/ibm_aml/HI-Small/train/transactions.csv`.
   - Source 2: First 150,000 transactions of `data/processed/ibm_aml/LI-Medium/train/transactions.csv`.
   - Combined Size: **300,000 rows** ($15$ laundering positives, prevalence $0.0050\%$).
2. **Validation Split**:
   - Source 1: First 50,000 transactions of `data/processed/ibm_aml/HI-Small/validation/transactions.csv`.
   - Source 2: First 50,000 transactions of `data/processed/ibm_aml/LI-Medium/validation/transactions.csv`.
   - Combined Size: **100,000 rows** ($78$ laundering positives, prevalence $0.0780\%$).
3. **Test Split**:
   - Source 1: First 50,000 transactions of `data/processed/ibm_aml/HI-Small/test/transactions.csv`.
   - Source 2: First 50,000 transactions of `data/processed/ibm_aml/LI-Medium/test/transactions.csv`.
   - Combined Size: **100,000 rows** ($94$ laundering positives, prevalence $0.0940\%$).

## 2. Integrity Verification

1. **Chronological Sorting**:
   - The merged records were sorted strictly by ascending `timestamp` via mergesort (`kind='mergesort'`).
   - Timestamps are strictly monotonic increasing with zero backward time travel.
2. **Schema & Label Semantics**:
   - Features, column names, column order, and label mapping are 100% identical to HI-Small and LI-Medium.
   - Target column `is_laundering` has identical binary semantics ($1 = \text{Laundering}$, $0 = \text{Legitimate}$).
3. **Subgroup Provenance**:
   - The column `dataset_source` was tagged during construction (`hi_small` vs `li_medium`) to allow subgroup auditing.
4. **Independent vs Overlapping Evaluation**:
   - Because Combined draws its rows from the initial slices of HI-Small and LI-Medium, it represents a composite multi-domain benchmark.
   - For holdout generalization testing, the full untouched test splits of HI-Small ($1,015,564$ rows) and LI-Medium ($6,249,112$ rows) remain available as independent gold-standard evaluations.
